ODL detection methodology: transactions classified as theoretically ODL-relevant when XRP purchased at a known origin exchange is sent across the XRPL and sold at a known destination exchange within ~60 seconds. Classification is estimated — not officially confirmed by Ripple. Methodology adapted from Utility-Scan (2019–2022). Corridor volume proportions: total ~$41M/day based on Ripple's confirmed >$15B annual ODL (2024); APAC ~56% of volume; USD→MXN via Bitso confirmed at ~$2B/year in 2024. Per-corridor breakdowns are estimated proportionally — Ripple does not publish corridor-level data. Small intraday volume variance (±4%) is applied at each refresh to reflect normal fluctuation around the daily average. No 24h change percentage is shown — we have no real-time delta data source and displaying invented figures would be misleading.

We use the conservative 2024 confirmed baseline. This model is illustrative — Ripple does not publish official per-corridor ODL breakdowns.

Important assumption: These calculations use live 24h exchange trading volume as a proxy for executable market depth — a simplification. True single-ticket execution capacity at any moment is lower than daily volume suggests, as order book depth is a fraction of turnover. The figures are best read as order-of-magnitude indicators, not precise limits. The "Mature market" column holds volume fixed while reducing volatility; in practice a mature market would also have higher volume, so the table underestimates capacity improvement at scale. The benchmark cell (10bp, today's σ) is the most defensible figure; other cells show directional sensitivity.
Sensitivity table: Q_max = V × (tolerance ÷ σ)² where V = live 24h exchange volume from CoinGecko. Daily exchange volume is used as a proxy for executable market depth — a simplifying assumption; true single-ticket depth at any moment is a fraction of daily turnover. These figures are order-of-magnitude indicators. Tolerance tiers: 5bp = sovereign/central bank standard, 10bp = institutional settlement benchmark (Part I), 50bp = remittance/retail outer bound. Volatility columns: 5% = current market, 3% = partial institutional adoption, 2% = deeply embedded settlement infrastructure. The "Mature market" column holds volume fixed — in practice higher price adoption would also produce higher volume, so those cells understate capacity at scale.
